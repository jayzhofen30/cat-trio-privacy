Cat Trio: Merge Adventure — Support

For the offline iOS and iPadOS version, contact Ruiqi Zhou at cattriogame@gmail.com.

Report a problem

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, game version, and what you did just before the problem appeared. A screenshot is optional. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or a full game save. If a child needs help, a parent or guardian should contact us and avoid including the child’s identifying details.

Game progress

This version saves progress on your device. It does not have account sign-in or cloud saves, and does not sync progress between devices. Deleting the app removes its local game data. Support cannot recover a deleted local save.

Saving cat pictures

In Collection, open an available photo and choose Save to device. The game asks only for permission to add the selected picture to Photos; it does not read your photo library. If permission was denied, you can change it in iOS or iPadOS Settings. You can continue playing without granting this permission.

Saving a picture to Photos does not back up the game. Pictures already saved to Photos remain independent of the game and can be deleted in Photos.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy for this iOS and iPadOS version. The policy is also available from Settings in the game. Sending support email is optional; we use it to respond and investigate the issue.
