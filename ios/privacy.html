Privacy Policy

Cat Trio: Merge Adventure — iPhone version with optional purchases

Last updated: November 10, 2026

About this policy

Cat Trio: Merge Adventure is provided by Ruiqi Zhou. This policy describes the iOS and iPadOS version with offline play, optional in-app purchases, and optional saving of game pictures to Photos, optional analytics and crash reports, and a backup of your game progress in your own iCloud. Account sign-in and advertisements are unavailable in this build. It does not describe other versions with different features.

Your game progress

Your board, scores, cats, journey progress, tools, and settings are saved on your device. When you are signed in to iCloud, the app also keeps a copy of that save, including the record of tools you bought, in the iCloud key-value storage of your own Apple Account, so you can continue after reinstalling the app or on another iPhone or iPad. Apple stores this copy under its own terms; it is never sent to us and we cannot read it. The game asks before restoring a backup and keeps a copy of the save it replaces on the device. If iCloud is off, progress stays only on the device, deleting the app removes it, and it cannot be recovered by contacting support. You can turn off iCloud for this app or delete its data in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

In-app purchases

You can buy packs of game tools and a permanent upgrade. Purchases are optional; the whole game can be played without paying. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your card details, Apple Account, name, or billing address. The app checks Apple’s signed purchase confirmation on your device and records the purchase (product, transaction number, and date) in your local game save so the tools are not lost or granted twice. We do not run a purchase server. Refunds and payment questions are handled by Apple under its own terms. Deleting the app removes the local purchase record; the permanent upgrade can be restored with Restore purchases in the shop.

Information used during play

You can play without providing a name, email address, or account. There is no advertising. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone for gameplay.

Optional analytics and crash reports

Analytics and crash reports are off until you select “I am 16 or older, allow” in the system alert. Select “Do not allow” if you are under 16 or do not want analytics; no analytics or crash reports are collected unless you allow them. If you allow them, the app sends information to Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics), which processes it for us. This can include: how you use the game, such as actions, progress and settings; information about purchases made in the app and their results, but not payment details; app sessions, versions and updates; crash reports and other diagnostics, such as stack traces, errors and performance; device information, such as model and operating system; approximate location derived from your connection; and random identifiers created for this installation. We use this information to understand how the game is played, to find and fix problems, and to improve the game and its shop. It is tied to those random identifiers, not to your name. We do not ask for or send your name or email address. Your consent choice stays on your device. We do not ask for or send your age. We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell it. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings: this stops new collection, clears local analytics data and asks Firebase to delete crash reports that have not been sent; a report that is already being sent may still arrive. Turning it off does not delete information Google has already received; to ask for deletion, email cattriogame@gmail.com.

Saving game pictures

When you choose Save to device, the app asks for permission to add the selected game picture to Photos. The app does not read your photo library or send pictures to us. Declining this permission does not prevent ordinary play. You can change the permission in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Pictures already saved to Photos remain there independently of the game and can be deleted in Photos. Your own Apple or iCloud Photos settings may sync these pictures; the game does not provide that sync.

Sharing pictures and ratings

When you choose Share photo, the game hands the selected picture to the iOS share sheet, and you pick where it goes. The game does not see who you share with and does not send the picture to us. The app may show Apple’s standard rating prompt once; iOS decides whether it appears, and any rating goes to Apple, not to us. No reward is tied to sharing or rating.

Children and families

The game is suitable for players aged 9 and older. Players under 16 should select “Do not allow” in the system alert. No analytics or crash reports are collected when analytics is not allowed. If a child needs help, a parent or guardian should contact support on their behalf and avoid sending the child’s name or other identifying details.

If you contact support

You may choose to email cattriogame@gmail.com. We receive your email address and any information or attachments you include, and use them to respond to your request and investigate problems. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary personal information. Support email is handled through Gmail, which processes email under Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting information

Local game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Analytics information and crash reports are kept for a limited period under the retention settings of Google Analytics and Firebase, and then deleted; reports built from them are aggregated. The iCloud backup stays in your iCloud until you delete it in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Deleting the app does not delete pictures you previously saved to Photos. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request and any follow-up. You may ask us to delete support correspondence by emailing cattriogame@gmail.com; information that must be retained for legal obligations may be kept.

Apple and your device

Apple, the App Store, TestFlight, iOS, and iPadOS may separately process information when you download or test apps, under their own settings and privacy policies. This policy describes the game’s own practices, not all processing by your device or Apple.

Reading the web copy

If you read a web copy of this policy hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. The offline copy opened from Settings in this iOS and iPadOS version does not load that webpage.

Policy updates and contact

We will update this policy when the game’s features or data practices change. The latest version is published at jayzhofen30.github.io/cat-trio-privacy/ios/privacy.html. You can read the policy from Settings in the app. For privacy questions or requests, contact Ruiqi Zhou at cattriogame@gmail.com.
