Privacy Policy

Cat Trio: Merge Adventure — iOS and iPadOS offline version

Last updated: October 22, 2026

About this policy

Cat Trio: Merge Adventure is provided by Ruiqi Zhou. This policy describes the iOS and iPadOS version with offline play and optional saving of game pictures to Photos. Account sign-in, cloud saves, in-app purchases, and advertisements are unavailable in this build. It does not describe other versions with different features.

Your game progress

Your board, scores, cats, journey progress, tools, and settings are saved on your device. This build does not send that saved progress to us or provide a cloud backup. Deleting the app removes its local game data. Local progress cannot be recovered by contacting support.

Information used during play

You can play without providing a name, email address, or account. This build does not include advertising or analytics services, and does not send gameplay activity or advertising identifiers to us. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone for gameplay.

Saving game pictures

When you choose Save to device, the app asks for permission to add the selected game picture to Photos. The app does not read your photo library or send pictures to us. Declining this permission does not prevent ordinary play. You can change the permission in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Pictures already saved to Photos remain there independently of the game and can be deleted in Photos. Your own Apple or iCloud Photos settings may sync these pictures; the game does not provide that sync.

Children and families

The game is suitable for players aged 9 and older. The offline game does not ask children for personal information. If a child needs help, a parent or guardian should contact support on their behalf and avoid sending the child’s name or other identifying details.

If you contact support

You may choose to email cattriogame@gmail.com. We receive your email address and any information or attachments you include, and use them to respond to your request and investigate problems. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary personal information. Support email is handled through Gmail, which processes email under Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting information

Local game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Deleting the app does not delete pictures you previously saved to Photos. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request and any follow-up. You may ask us to delete support correspondence by emailing cattriogame@gmail.com; information that must be retained for legal obligations may be kept.

Apple and your device

Apple, the App Store, TestFlight, iOS, and iPadOS may separately process information when you download or test apps, under their own settings and privacy policies. This policy describes the game’s own practices, not all processing by your device or Apple.

Reading the web copy

If you read a web copy of this policy hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. The offline copy opened from Settings in this iOS and iPadOS version does not load that webpage.

Policy updates and contact

We will update this policy when the game’s features or data practices change. You can read the policy from Settings in the app. For privacy questions or requests, contact Ruiqi Zhou at cattriogame@gmail.com.
